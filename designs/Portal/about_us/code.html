Our Mission & Vision

Harmonizing Legislation for a Healthier Region

The SADC Parliamentary Forum is dedicated to advancing Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR) through robust policy frameworks, ensuring every individual in the region has access to the care and information they deserve.

Our History & Purpose

Established with the conviction that parliamentary action is critical to sustainable development, the SADC PF serves as a unified voice for member parliaments. Our purpose within the SRHR domain is to transcend borders, building consensus on critical health issues.

We work to bridge the gap between global commitments and local realities, fostering an environment where legislation protects and empowers vulnerable populations, particularly women and girls, across Southern Africa.

Our Approach

Three pillars driving systemic change in SRHR.

Advocacy

We amplify the need for progressive SRHR legislation, engaging stakeholders at all levels to build political will and community support.

Policy Monitoring

Rigorous tracking of legislative implementation ensures accountability and measures the real-world impact of SRHR policies.

Knowledge Sharing

Facilitating the exchange of best practices and research among member states to accelerate regional progress in health outcomes.

Regional Reach

Our initiatives span across the Southern African Development Community, uniting nations under a common goal of comprehensive sexual and reproductive health.
